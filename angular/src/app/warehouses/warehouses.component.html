Facility Network Architecture
How physical storage facilities translate into scannable barcodes, inventory batches, and FEFO picking routes.
Step 1
🏢 Warehouse Facility

The overall central distribution hub or fulfillment center (e.g., {{ selectedWarehouse.name }}). Holds geographic address, square footage, and regional compliance.

Step 2
📦 Storage Zones

Designated temperature or security chambers inside the facility. E.g., Cold Chain (2°C - 8°C), Ambient Pallet Storage, Hazardous chemicals, or Quarantine.

Step 3
🏷️ Barcode Bins

4-coordinate physical bin locations (Aisle-Rack-Shelf-Bin). Workers scan this barcode with hand-held mobile terminals during putaway and picking.

Step 4
📊 Stock Inventory

Individual product SKUs, manufacturing lots, expiry dates, and serialized units assigned directly to each scannable bin location for live tracking.
