Facility Network Architecture

Understanding how physical warehouses translate into digital barcodes, inventory lots, and picking routes.

Step 1
🏢 Warehouse Facility

The overall physical campus or building (e.g. {{ selectedWarehouse.name }}). Holds address, capacity, and city hub details.

Step 2
📦 Storage Zones

Physical chambers inside the building. E.g., Cold Chain (2°C - 8°C), Ambient Pallet Reserve, Mezzanine, or Quarantine.

Step 3
🏷️ Barcode Bins

4-coordinate scannable slots (Aisle, Rack, Shelf, Bin). Putaway workers scan this code on mobile handheld scanners.

Step 4
📊 Stock Inventory

Specific product SKUs, batches, expiry dates, and quantities stored directly into these scannable bins.
